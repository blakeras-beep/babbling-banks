## Overview

**Direction: Banks Originals.** The brand is built from Banks' own artwork, a tape-resist crayon drawing: lemon and cobalt crayon rubbed over masking tape, the tape peeled away to leave crisp white strips, a few thin wandering crayon lines through it. Everything decorative comes from that one piece. The art frames the screen, the white tape strips carry the content, crayon marks what matters, and the words do the joking.

Babbling Banks (babblingbanks.com) finds a toddler TV episode from whatever the kid said: "the one with the monkey and the bike" becomes the actual episode. It's named for Banks.

- **Audience:** parents and grown-up caregivers of 1–5 year olds, on a phone, one-handed, usually mid-request. The brand speaks to the grown-up; the art is the kid's.
- **Personality:** handmade, quick, funny, warm, useful. **Not:** babyish, cartoon-mascot, pastel ed-tech, "playful brand kit", or generic rounded-sans kid app.
- **Peers in spirit:** a fridge covered in real kid art, a good picture-book jacket. **Anti-peers:** Memphis and neo-brutalist kits (scattered primitive shapes, colored dots, sticker badges), clip-art crayons, AI-generated "kid style" art, the old Babbling Banks (teal + coral, emoji) and the v0.2 ink-and-yellow minimal rebuild.

**Defining moves**
1. **Content lives on tape.** Every content container is a white `surface-tape` strip: crisp rectangle, no radius, no shadow, no outline. It is the white left behind when the tape comes off.
2. **Crayon frames, never fills.** The tape-resist art sits behind the top of home and results and on share cards. Text never sits directly on crayon; it sits on tape over it.
3. **Lemon crayon means "this matched".** Matched words in the kid's quote get a lemon crayon swipe behind them. Nothing else on screen is lemon except the art.
4. **Crayon draws the connections.** A hand-drawn ink crayon arrow runs from the kid's words to the answer with a small "found it" tape label; thin cobalt crayon lines are the only dividers and underlines.
5. **One wordmark, no ornaments.** "babbling banks" in DynaPuff 600. No dot, no badge, no icon beside it.
6. **Credit the artist, quietly, by name.** "Art by Banks · <piece title>", small, bottom-right, once per page.

**Precedence when sources conflict:** this DESIGN.md > approved examples in `examples/` > the live site or code > anything else.

## Typography

Three families plus a mono for data. Each has one job.

- **DynaPuff 600:** the wordmark and short playful labels only ("found it", "or maybe…", "try one"). Never sentences, never above 26 px except the wordmark, never 700 (it swells).
- **Dela Gothic One:** the headline, the kid's quote, episode titles, the primary button. Its blunt shapes read like cut letters.
- **Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400/700:** body, inputs, chips, metadata, the credit. Chosen for legibility at arm's length; it is the quiet voice so the art and Dela can be loud.
- **IBM Plex Mono 500:** episode codes (`S2E14`) and song timestamps only.

## Layout

- **Mobile (default, 375 px):** single column, 18 px side margins. **≥600 px:** 48 px side margins, content column max 560 px, centered. Field pieces stay full-bleed across the top, so the art frames the column on both sides; object pieces hang whole, centered over the column, at most 440 px tall. No wide layout in v1.
- **Spacing:** 8 pt scale.
- **Home rhythm:** header 56 → art band (Blue and Yellow hung whole: full width, natural height, about 282 px at 375) → headline strip overlapping the band's bottom by 62 → 20 → lead → 20 → search field → 6 → cobalt crayon underline → 22 → primary button → 22 → "try one" → example searches → credit.
- **Results rhythm:** header → art band behind the top 430 px → quote strip → arrow row (84 px: arrow + "found it" label) → result card → 26 → "or maybe…" → rows → credit.
- **Rotation (the handmade tilt):** only the headline strip (−2.5°), quote strip (−2°), result card (+1°), primary button (+1.5°) and the "found it" label (−4°) rotate. Everything else is 0°. Tilt reads as handmade; tilted inputs and lists slow reading and hurt tap accuracy.
- **No decoration in empty space.** Paper below the content stays paper. Reason: scattered shapes and doodles in leftover space are the strongest "playful template" tell.

## Elevation & Depth

None. Tape sits flat on paper or art; layering comes from overlap and rotation, never shadow. No `box-shadow` anywhere. Mobile sheets are full-screen `surface` with a 40% ink scrim behind.

## Shapes

- `none` (0 px) on everything rectangular: tape strips, buttons, fields, cards, thumbnails. Tape is cut, not rounded.
- `full` only for the mic button's tap circle.
- **Crayon marks** (all transparent PNGs in `assets/art/`, made with the same wax-on-paper-tooth method as the hero art):
  - `crayon-swipe-lemon.png` / `-2.png`: the match highlight, stretched behind a word from 0.18em outside each side, top at 0.12em, bottom at −0.08em.
  - `crayon-line-cobalt.png`: dividers (10 px tall, 85% of the content width) and the search-field underline (14 px, full width).
  - `crayon-arrow-ink.png`: the one connector from quote to result, 86 × 82 px.
- No cut-paper shapes, no colored dots, no sticker badges, no hairlines.

## Components

**Header** · 56 px, `surface`. `wordmark.svg` left at 24 px tall (links home), heart icon right (24 px Phosphor Bold in a 48 px target, label "Repeat offenders"). On detail screens a CaretLeft back button (48 px target) sits left of the wordmark. No bottom rule. The header is exempt from the full clear-space rule; keep at least 16 px clear around the wordmark.

**Art band** · one of Banks' pieces, full-bleed, decorative (`alt=""`; the credit names it). How it fills the band depends on the piece's mode (see Art modes): a **field** piece crops with `object-fit: cover` at its focus point, 430 px behind results and the empty screen, 120 px on detail; an **object** piece is shown whole at full width and natural height (home: Blue and Yellow, about 282 px at 375; 404: Black and Blue, about 254 px), never cropped. In dark theme `night-01` (a field) crops to the same height the light piece takes, so nothing shifts when the theme changes. Each screen has its own piece (see Photography & Imagery), so moving through the app feels like walking past different pictures on the fridge. Dark theme: every band uses `night-01`, except the 404, which keeps `scribble-01-cutout.png` inverted (`filter: invert(1) hue-rotate(180deg)`) so the lines read paper-colored on night.

**Spot art** · for the in-between states (loading, offline). A cutout of a piece drawn on white paper (`leaf-01-cutout.png`, `cone-01-cutout.png`, transparent PNG), 240 px tall and at most 70% of the strip's width, centered at the top of a straight tape strip, with the Dela 22 line and body below it, centered. Always on white tape, so it needs no dark-theme variant. One spot per screen, never in empty space outside a strip.

**Tape strip** · `surface-tape`, no radius, no shadow, 16 × 18 px padding. Variants: headline (−2.5°, overlaps the art band), quote (−2°), result card (+1°), field (0°), found label (−4°).

**Search field** · straight tape strip, 56 px min height, `input` type, placeholder in `text-muted` ("the dog pool one", 18 characters max), mic button right (48 px target). The cobalt crayon line sits 6 px below and defines the field against the paper. Focus: 2 px ink outline offset 3 px. Error: the line is replaced by a 3 px `danger` rule and helper text below in `danger` caption. Long queries wrap; Enter submits.

**Primary button** · solid `primary` ink block, `button` type in `on-primary`, 18 × 34 px padding, rotated 1.5°, 56 px min height. Hover: fill `#34333A`. Pressed: rotate to 0°, translate 2 px down. Focus: 2 px ink outline offset 3 px. Disabled: `surface-sunk` fill, `text-muted`. Loading: "Decoding…". One per view. The label says what the link does: "Play this one" only when it opens the video itself (a YouTube video, an episode with its own URL); "Open on Disney+" (the show's platform) when it opens a platform search page. Reason: a solid block reads as tappable; a crayon swipe did not.

**Secondary button** · straight tape strip with a 2 px ink outline, `label` type: "Not it? Add a clue". One per view; a second action beside it ("8 more" on results) is a text action in `label` type with no outline.

**Example search** · the kid's words in real quotes, `label` type in `crayon-cobalt`, no container, no icon, 44 px min tap height, stacked. 24 characters max. Introduced by the playful label "try one".

**Translation pair** (signature)
1. *Quote strip:* the kid's words in `quote` type. Each matched word (max three) gets a lemon crayon swipe behind it, stretched to the word's width; alternate `crayon-swipe-lemon.png` and `-2.png`. No swipe when nothing matched.
2. *Arrow row:* `crayon-arrow-ink.png` (image box 86 × 82 px) from the quote down to the card, with the "found it" label beside its head: a small tape strip, DynaPuff 18 in `crayon-cobalt`, 7 × 10 px padding, rotated −4°. No exclamation mark.
3. *Result card:* episode title in `headline`, caption row (show name in cobalt, `S2E14` in mono, runtime in ink), official thumbnail 16:9 with no radius (placeholder `surface-sunk`).

**Result row** · title in `title`, caption below in `text-muted` with show and code (show names are cobalt only on the result card), heart right in a 48 px target. Rows are separated by a cobalt crayon line (10 px tall, 85% width). Max four. No markers.

**Section label** · `playful` type, ink: "or maybe…", "try one", "songs in this one".

**Empty state** · the results screen minus the arrow, "found it" and rows: header → art band (`stars-01`) → the kid's quote strip (no swipes) with a "You searched" caption → a straight tape strip holding the Dela 20 line "That one stumped us.", the body line "Try a character, a color, or a song they keep humming." and three example searches under "try one" → one secondary button "Say it another way" (refocuses the search field) → credit. No decoration.

**Error** · inline under the field in `danger` caption with a WarningCircle icon. No modal.

**Credit** · "Art by Banks · <piece title>", `credit` type in `text-muted`, bottom-right of the page content, 16 px from the right and 12 px from the bottom. Once per page. The title is the piece that screen shows, from `assets/art/collection.json` (home "Blue and Yellow", results "Mint and Blue", dark screens "Emerald Texture", and so on); a page showing several pieces uses plain "Art by Banks". It links to the art page (no underline until hover); the art page itself carries no credit, because the whole page is one. Keep the title under 20 characters so the line stays short; every current title fits.

**Theme toggle** · "night" / "day" as a text button at the bottom-left of the credit row, `caption` type in `text-muted`, in a 48 px target flush with the gutter. Light is the standard; the toggle turns "night" on or off and the choice is remembered on the device.

**Pager** (episode detail) · previous and next episode as text actions with a caret, the word "previous" or "next" in `caption` `text-muted` above the title so a lone link still reads.

**Wall label** · on the art page, under each piece: the title in `playful` 13 px ink, the medium below it in Atkinson 11 `text-muted` ("finger paint, foil stars"), 6 px below the piece.

## Do's and Don'ts

- **Do** put text on white tape. **Don't** set text directly on crayon; it breaks legibility and buries the art.
- **Do** use lemon crayon only for matched words. **Don't** put lemon on buttons, tags or backgrounds; it stops meaning "this matched".
- **Do** use the cobalt crayon line for dividers. **Don't** use hairlines, black bars or shadows; they read as a kit.
- **Do** leave empty paper empty. **Don't** add shapes, dots, doodles or stickers to fill space; that is the template tell.
- **Do** tilt only the five listed elements. **Don't** tilt inputs, rows or body text.
- **Do** keep DynaPuff to the wordmark and short labels. **Don't** set headlines or sentences in it.
- **Do** use Banks' actual pieces and credit them. **Don't** generate "in the style of Banks" art and call it his; the credit must be true.
- **Do** crop field pieces at their focus point and show object pieces whole. **Don't** crop the paper out of an object piece; the white is part of the picture.
- **Do** name the piece in the credit. **Don't** leave a titled piece as anonymous decoration.
- **Do** crop or retouch the handwritten "Banks" signature out of UI art; the credit already names him. **Don't** show it inside the app (print is fine).
- **Do** keep show characters out of the art. **Don't** use pieces with character stickers (`10 Crayon and Stickers`) unless the stickers are removed by hand from the original.
- **Do** use the SVG wordmark. **Don't** add a dot, badge or icon to it.
- **Do** use `#F5F6F2` paper. **Don't** use cream, beige or pure white as the page ground.

Misuse: (1) adding a dot, badge or icon, (2) title case or caps, (3) DynaPuff 700 or another font, (4) outlines, shadows or gradients, (5) placing directly on crayon art, (6) stretching, (7) rotating the letters (the tape strip under it may tilt; the wordmark doesn't).

- **New art:** only from Banks' real pieces, photographed flat in daylight or scanned, then cleaned up. Each new piece gets a numbered file (`tape-resist-02`), its print title in this table, and earns the credit.
- **Episode thumbnails:** official images as supplied, 16:9, no radius, no filters.
- **Never:** AI-generated characters or people, stock family photos, clip-art crayons, show characters drawn or traced.

## Voice & Tone

Playful and silly, aimed at the grown-up. The joke is the shared chaos of a tiny critic; never at the kid's expense.

| This | Not that |
|---|---|
| "Say it exactly how they said it. Mumbles, sound effects, all of it." | "Enter a description" |
| "That one stumped us. Try a character, a color, or a song they keep humming." | "Oops! No results found!" |
| "Repeat offenders" | "My Favorites" |
| "found it" | "Match found!" |
| "Not it? Add a clue" | "Refine search" |

- **Home headline:** "Find it from whatever they said." It replaced "Toddler in. Episode out." after review flagged the "X in. Y out." slogan shape. The title tag and share titles carry the same line. Still worth testing a line in the toddler's own words (a real quote like "the one with the pool").
- **Banned:** elevate, seamless, unlock, empower, journey, magic/magical, smart, AI-powered, mommy/mama (say parents or grown-ups), baby-talk spellings, screen-time judgment.
- **Punctuation:** no exclamation marks in UI. Em dashes at most one per ~150 words, never in headlines.
- **CTAs:** verb + outcome, 2–4 words, and never promise more than the link does: "Find it", "Play this one" (opens the video), "Open on Disney+" (opens a platform search), "Share the decode", "Report it on GitHub".
- **The banned list applies to data too.** Show names and descriptions in `shows.json` are copy the app renders; `npm run validate-catalog` checks them. Episode synopses quote their sources and are exempt.

## Known Gaps

- Icons are stock Phosphor; the most ownable next step is icons drawn by Banks and recreated.
- The list row, header and search field are standard patterns by necessity; a fresh review still reads them as kit parts (overall 5/10 "generic" after v1.1, down from 6/10). The art and copy carry the brand; the UI designer can push components further toward crayon.
- The masters are cleaned-up versions of photos of the originals, not flat scans. If a piece ever needs to print larger than its print size, rescan the original.
- Cutout PNGs are about 1.2 MB each; serve them as WebP or AVIF in production.
- Rights: show names and platform names appear as link labels ("Open on Disney+", "on Paramount+"); confirm thumbnail, show-name and platform-name use against each platform's terms; confirm COPPA posture before any kid-facing feature. Not legal advice.
